All figures in this directory use a 10-year sliding window stepped annually
(win10y_step12), climatology-anomaly detrending, a manually-set 2000-01
changepoint, and n=1000 phase-surrogate resamples for significance testing,
on VODCA CX+Ku for the ten water-supply catchments (vod-all).

vod-all_win10y_step12_climatology_split200001_nsurr1000.png
-------------------------------------------------------------
Grid of one column per catchment x one row per requested metric (AC-1,
Variance), each panel showing that catchment's sliding-window time series
with the 2000-01 changepoint marked as a vertical dashed line.

multisite_ac1_var_win10y_step12_climatology_split200001_nsurr1000.png
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) AC-1 and (b) normalized variance time series for all ten catchments
overlaid on one shared axis per metric, each catchment colored by its fixed
per-catchment magma color and the 2000-01 changepoint marked per catchment.
The primary multi-catchment comparison figure.

ac1_grid_win10y_step12_climatology_split200001_nsurr1000.png
------------------------------------------------------------
5x2 grid, one panel per catchment, of the AC-1 (lag-1 autocorrelation)
sliding-window time series. All lines share one fixed color (matching the
AC-1 line in ac1_var_grid) since catchment identity is already given by each
panel's title.

ac1_var_grid_win10y_step12_climatology_split200001_nsurr1000.png
------------------------------------------------------------------
5x2 grid, one panel per catchment, overlaying AC-1 (left axis) and
normalized variance (right axis) on twin y-axes. Variance is normalized by
each catchment's full-record residual standard deviation so it is
comparable in dimensionless units across catchments with different native
scales; AC-1 needs no such normalization since it is already a scale-free
ratio (a lag-1 autocorrelation/AR(1) coefficient) by construction — rescaling
the input leaves it unchanged.

ac1_changepoint_grid_win10y_step12_climatology_split200001_nsurr1000.png
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
5x2 grid of AC-1 time series per catchment. The 2000-01 changepoint is
marked with a dashed vertical line, and the background is shaded red where
the pre- or post-split segment trend is significantly increasing (phase-
surrogate test, p<0.05), blue where significantly decreasing. Six catchments
show a significant increasing trend post-2000: (a) Hinckley, NY (τ=+0.75,
p=0.00), (b) Cannonsville, NY (τ=+0.59, p=0.02), (c) Sebago, ME (τ=+0.76,
p=0.00), (e) Scituate, RI (τ=+0.68, p=0.02), (h) Quabbin, MA (τ=+0.96,
p=0.00), and (j) Massabesic, NH (τ=+0.79, p=0.00). Seven catchments show a
significant decreasing trend pre-2000: (a) Hinckley, NY (τ=-0.86, p=0.01),
(b) Cannonsville, NY (τ=-0.86, p=0.01), (d) Alcove, NY (τ=-0.79, p=0.02),
(g) Barkhamsted, CT (τ=-0.79, p=0.02), (h) Quabbin, MA (τ=-0.93, p=0.00),
(i) Wachusett, MA (τ=-0.79, p=0.02), and (j) Massabesic, NH (τ=-0.79,
p=0.01).

pre_post_arrows_vod_win10y_step12_climatology_split200001_nsurr1000.png
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Dot-and-arrow chart, one row per catchment: a dot at the pre-2000 Kendall
tau (AC-1), a dot at the post-2000 tau, and an arrow connecting them (pre =
dark purple, post = orange). A dot is filled if that segment's trend is
significant (phase-surrogate p<0.05), open otherwise; the connecting arrow
is solid only when both endpoints are significant, dotted/faded otherwise.
Background shading marks decreasing (tau<0) vs. increasing (tau>0)
autocorrelation.

pre_post_scatter_win10y_step12_climatology_split200001_nsurr1000.png
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scatter of each catchment's pre-2000 Kendall tau (AC-1, x-axis) against its
post-2000 tau (y-axis). The upper-left quadrant (dec -> inc) is the regime-
shift signature: decreasing autocorrelation pre-2000, increasing after.
Marker fill indicates whether the post-2000 trend is significant under the
phase-surrogate test; each catchment's label is colored to match its fixed
palette color.

to (b) the per-catchment pre/post dot-and-arrow chart (same as
pre_post_arrows_vod), combined side by side for direct comparison.
